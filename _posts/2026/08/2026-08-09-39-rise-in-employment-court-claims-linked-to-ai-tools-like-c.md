Britain's employment courts have seen a **39 percent increase** in claims through March 2026, attributed to the use of AI tools like **ChatGPT** and **Grok** for writing claims. This surge has resulted in a **55 percent increase** in the backlog of unresolved cases, totaling **64,000** pending cases. The situation has been described as a "tragedy of the commons, AI edition" by **The Economist**. This influx of AI-generated claims is straining the judicial system, impacting the speed and efficiency of case resolutions. Practitioners in the legal field may need to adapt their strategies to manage the growing volume of AI-assisted litigation.
