NVIDIA has launched NVLink Fusion, integrating NVHBM (Custom High-Bandwidth Memory) to address the demands of trillion-parameter workloads in AI infrastructure. This technology aims to optimize the performance of AI systems by unifying compute, memory, storage, networking, and software, as stated by NVIDIA. The introduction of NVHBM is part of the next wave of AI infrastructure evolution, reflecting current trends in the industry. Practitioners can expect improved efficiency in handling large-scale AI tasks. This follows NVIDIA's recent advancements in AI technologies, including the Groq 3 LPX launch, which emphasizes the importance of integrated systems for AI performance, as reported by the [NVIDIA Blog](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/nvlink-fusion-nvhbm-custom-high-bandwidth-memory/).
