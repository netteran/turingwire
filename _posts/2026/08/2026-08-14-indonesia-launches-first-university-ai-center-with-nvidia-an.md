Indonesia's first university-based AI technology center has been launched by Universitas Gadjah Mada (UGM) in collaboration with Indosat Ooredoo Hutchison and NVIDIA. This initiative aims to develop local AI talent and is supported by the Ministry of Communication and Digital Affairs. The center will focus on enhancing AI education and research capabilities within Indonesia. This follows NVIDIA's ongoing commitment to advancing AI infrastructure in the region, as highlighted in previous coverage. The establishment of this center signifies a strategic move for Indonesia to take charge of its AI future, as stated by a source involved in the project. For practitioners, this development opens opportunities for collaboration and innovation in AI applications within the Indonesian market. More details can be found on the [NVIDIA Blog](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/ugm-indosat-nvidia-ai-technology-center/).
