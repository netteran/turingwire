OpenAI's first smart speaker is expected to be released in 2027, with a price point above $300. CEO Sam Altman stated that the device is designed to learn from conversations and adapt to users. This marks OpenAI's entry into the smart speaker market, potentially impacting how users interact with AI in their daily environments. For developers, this could open up new avenues for integrating conversational AI into consumer products. The announcement follows OpenAI's recent enhancements in AI capabilities, including the launch of ChatGPT Work and Codex plugins for educators and students, indicating a broader strategy to embed AI into everyday tools.
