OpenAI has announced it will wind down its contract providing AI models to Cursor, which was recently acquired by SpaceX. This decision follows the acquisition, although the specific date of the acquisition has not been disclosed. The move indicates a shift in Cursor's operational framework under SpaceX, impacting its access to OpenAI's technology. This follows previous discussions on AI security risks and the implications of faster AI models, highlighting the evolving landscape of AI partnerships. For developers relying on Cursor's integration of OpenAI models, this change may necessitate adjustments in their project strategies. More details are available on the [OpenAI Blog](https://openai.com/index/our-decision-on-cursor-following-its-acquisition-by-spacex).
