Chinese state-backed hacking groups have more than doubled their attacks since they began using AI models like DeepSeek to write exploit code and scan networks, according to TeamT5. This increase highlights the growing capabilities of cyber threats enhanced by AI tools such as ChatGPT and Claude Code. A UK study indicates that the cyber capabilities of open models are rapidly advancing, posing a significant risk to cybersecurity. Practitioners should be aware of the heightened threat landscape as these AI tools enable more sophisticated and frequent attacks. This follows previous discussions on the implications of AI in cybersecurity, emphasizing the urgent need for enhanced defensive measures against such threats.
