OpenAI has commenced testing advertisements within ChatGPT to sustain its free access model. The initiative aims to integrate ads while maintaining user experience through clear labeling, answer independence, strong privacy protections, and user control, as stated by OpenAI. This move marks a significant shift in monetization strategies for AI services, potentially affecting how users interact with the platform. This follows OpenAI's ongoing efforts to balance revenue generation with user experience, as highlighted in previous coverage on responsible AI infrastructure and finance functions. For developers and product managers, this change may influence how they design user interactions and monetization strategies in AI applications. More details are available in the [OpenAI Blog](https://openai.com/index/testing-ads-in-chatgpt).
