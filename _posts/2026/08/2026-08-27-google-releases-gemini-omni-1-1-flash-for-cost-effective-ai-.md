Google has launched the Gemini Omni 1.1 Flash video generation model, which allows for a maximum of 10 seconds of footage to be analyzed for scene extensions and supports up to 40 seconds of scene extension duration. The model achieves a 60% speed increase in 360p draft mode while reducing costs by one-third in the same mode. This release enhances the flexibility and affordability of AI video generation, making it more accessible for developers and content creators. The advancements in Gemini Omni 1.1 Flash follow Google's ongoing efforts to improve AI capabilities, as seen in their previous models.
