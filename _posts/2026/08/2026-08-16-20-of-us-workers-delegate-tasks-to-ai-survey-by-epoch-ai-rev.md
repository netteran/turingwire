20 percent of employed Americans now delegate at least one task to AI, according to a survey conducted by Epoch AI. The survey indicates that these workers generally accept AI output with little to no editing. This trend highlights a growing reliance on AI tools in the workplace, potentially impacting collaboration dynamics among teams. This follows ongoing discussions about AI's role in task delegation and productivity enhancements in various sectors, as seen in prior reports on AI integration in workplace settings. For practitioners, this suggests a shift in how task management and collaboration tools may need to evolve to accommodate AI assistance.
