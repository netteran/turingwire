Meta's Instagram has acknowledged that users often cannot distinguish between AI-generated profiles and real people. In response, the platform plans to implement a new labeling system by late 2024, replacing the 'AI creator' tag with an 'AI-generated profile' label. This move aims to clarify the presence of AI characters alongside human users, reflecting Meta's strategy for coexistence on the platform. The admission highlights ongoing challenges in user perception of AI integration in social media, as stated by Instagram. This follows Meta's broader efforts to enhance transparency in AI interactions, as seen in previous developments like the Muse Glimmer model. For AI developers, this change may influence how they design and present AI personas in social media contexts.
