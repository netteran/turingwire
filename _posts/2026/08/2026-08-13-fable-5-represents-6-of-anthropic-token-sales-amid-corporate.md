Fable 5, Anthropic's latest AI model, currently accounts for only 6% of the tokens sold by the company. This slow adoption raises concerns about corporate willingness to invest further in frontier AI technologies. An unverified claim suggests that corporate AI spending may have reached a ceiling, particularly if performance improvements do not yield measurable everyday value. This follows previous discussions on Anthropic's models and their implications for corporate use, highlighting a potential stagnation in the market for advanced AI solutions. Practitioners may need to reassess their investment strategies in AI technologies as corporate demand appears to be plateauing.
