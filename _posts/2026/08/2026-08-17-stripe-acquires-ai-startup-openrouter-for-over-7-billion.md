Stripe is acquiring OpenRouter for more than $7 billion, significantly above OpenRouter's latest valuation of $1.3 billion. OpenRouter boasts over 400 AI models and serves eight million users. The CEO of OpenRouter described Stripe as 'Stripe for AI,' indicating a strategic alignment in their offerings. This acquisition follows a trend where major companies are integrating AI capabilities to enhance their services, as seen in previous reports on AI adoption in various sectors. Practitioners can expect enhanced AI functionalities within Stripe's ecosystem as a result of this acquisition, which could streamline payment processing and related services.
