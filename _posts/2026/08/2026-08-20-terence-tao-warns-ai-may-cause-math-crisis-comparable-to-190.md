Terence Tao, a prominent mathematician, asserts that AI could instigate a crisis in mathematics akin to the foundational upheaval experienced around 1900. He emphasizes that any proof generated by AI that cannot be explained by humans should be deemed incomplete. This perspective raises concerns about the reliability of AI-generated mathematical proofs and their implications for the field. Tao's remarks highlight the potential for AI to disrupt traditional mathematical understanding, echoing historical shifts in the discipline. This follows ongoing discussions about AI's impact on various fields, including previous critiques of its role in medicine and programming communities, as reported by [The Decoder](https://the-decoder.com/terence-tao-says-ai-could-trigger-maths-biggest-crisis-since-godel/).
