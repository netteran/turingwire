OpenAI has initiated a stock buyback program allowing employees to cash out $7 billion in stock. This decision comes as the company, valued at $852 billion, aims to alleviate liquidity pressure for its workforce ahead of a potential IPO. In October 2025, OpenAI previously facilitated a stock sale amounting to $6.6 billion. The current move reflects ongoing efforts to provide financial flexibility to employees, as noted by an unverified claim regarding the motivation behind this buyback. This development is significant for employees and stakeholders as it indicates OpenAI's readiness for a public offering, which could reshape its financial landscape.
