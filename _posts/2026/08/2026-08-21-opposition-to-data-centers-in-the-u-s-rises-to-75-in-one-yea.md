Seventy-five percent of Americans now oppose having a data center built near them, a significant increase from 42% just one year ago, according to Heatmap News. Furthermore, 61% of respondents reported being 'strongly opposed' to such developments. This shift reflects growing concerns about the impact of data centers on local communities. The survey highlights a notable change in public sentiment, with three out of four Americans now against data centers, compared to an even split a year prior. This trend may influence future site selection and regulatory considerations for companies planning new data center projects, as public opposition could complicate approvals and community relations.
