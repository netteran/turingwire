Firebird has officially launched the largest AI factory in the CIS region today in Armenia. This facility is expected to leverage NVIDIA's accelerated computing technology and Dell Technologies' high-performance AI infrastructure. Prime Minister Nikol Pashinyan attended the inauguration, highlighting the significance of this development for the region's AI capabilities. This launch follows a trend of increasing investment in AI infrastructure across the CIS, as seen in previous initiatives. Practitioners can anticipate enhanced AI cloud services from Firebird as a result of this new facility, which aims to support a growing demand for AI solutions in the region.
