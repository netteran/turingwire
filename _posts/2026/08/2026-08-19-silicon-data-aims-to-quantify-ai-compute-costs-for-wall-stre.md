Silicon Data is developing solutions to help Wall Street quantify the costs associated with AI compute, which is the single biggest expense in building AI products. Annual spending on data centers and GPUs reaches hundreds of billions of dollars, highlighting the financial stakes involved. Despite this, an unnamed source claims that there still isn’t a straightforward way to put a price on compute. The current AI buildout shows no signs of slowing, making accurate pricing increasingly critical for investors and companies alike. This initiative follows a growing trend in the industry to better understand and manage AI-related costs, as highlighted in previous coverage by [TechCrunch AI](https://techcrunch.com/video/meet-the-startup-helping-wall-street-put-a-price-on-ai-compute/).
