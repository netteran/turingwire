Nvidia is negotiating an investment in Perplexity, which is currently valued at over $30 billion, representing a more than 50% increase from its last funding round. Perplexity has reported annualized revenue exceeding $750 million, with revenue growth tripling recently. This potential investment follows Nvidia's ongoing strategy to bolster its portfolio in the AI sector, as seen in previous reports regarding its investment adjustments in OpenAI and the rise in AI server prices amid a DRAM shortage. The outcome of these negotiations could influence the competitive landscape for AI-driven search technologies, particularly as Perplexity continues to expand its market presence, according to [The Decoder](https://the-decoder.com/nvidia-in-talks-to-invest-in-perplexity-at-30-billion-plus-valuation/).
