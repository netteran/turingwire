NVIDIA asserts that AI factories are the defining infrastructure of the AI era. These factories rely on several critical resources including advanced chips, packaging, memory, networking, land, power, and shell. As compute becomes a primary revenue driver in the AI economy, the demand for these resources is expected to grow significantly. This aligns with NVIDIA's ongoing focus on securing the necessary infrastructure to support AI advancements, following their recent investments and partnerships aimed at enhancing AI capabilities. Practitioners in the AI field should prepare for increased competition in sourcing these critical components, as they become essential for building and scaling AI solutions. More details can be found in the [NVIDIA Blog](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/securing-the-infrastructure-of-intelligence/).
