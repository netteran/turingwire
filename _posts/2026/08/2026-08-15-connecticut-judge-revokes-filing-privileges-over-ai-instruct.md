Judge Spader revoked the electronic filing privileges of a plaintiff who embedded invisible AI instructions in court documents using a 3-point white text format on a white background. This action was taken after the court identified the attempt to secretly tamper with a jury, as stated by Judge Spader. Notably, the court clarified that Connecticut does not utilize AI to review filings, highlighting the unusual nature of this case. This incident raises concerns about the integrity of court filings and the potential for AI misuse in legal contexts, as practitioners must now be vigilant against similar tactics.
