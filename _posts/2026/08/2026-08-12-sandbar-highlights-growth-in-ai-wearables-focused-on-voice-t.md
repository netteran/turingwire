Sandbar reports a significant increase in AI notetaking hardware over the past couple of years. The company emphasizes that a new wave of wearables, particularly rings, is emerging to help users capture stray thoughts and ideas seamlessly. Current device offerings include credit-card-sized gadgets, pendants, pins, and transcribing earbuds. This trend reflects a broader shift towards voice technology in wearables, which could influence future product development in the AI hardware space. The insights from Sandbar align with previous coverage of advancements in AI hardware, particularly in the context of enhancing user experience and functionality in everyday tasks, as noted by [TechCrunch AI](https://techcrunch.com/video/why-stream-ring-maker-sandbar-says-the-future-of-ai-wearables-is-voice/).
