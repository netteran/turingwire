Google's Nano Banana 2 image model was launched and then pulled within just two days due to concerns over misuse. The model enabled users to generate fake satellite imagery with minimal input; for instance, a simple prompt could create a scene depicting a refugee column at the Mexican border. This rapid removal highlights the challenges of managing AI tools that can easily produce misleading content. The incident follows ongoing discussions about the ethical implications of AI-generated media, as seen in previous coverage of Google's AI initiatives. Practitioners should be aware of the potential for swift action against tools that facilitate misinformation, as reported by [The Decoder](https://the-decoder.com/google-handed-users-the-easiest-possible-tool-for-fake-satellite-imagery-then-pulled-it-after-two-days/).
