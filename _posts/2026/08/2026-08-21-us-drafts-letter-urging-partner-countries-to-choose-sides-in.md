The United States is drafting a letter to partner countries instructing them to choose sides in the ongoing AI standoff with Beijing, as reported by Reuters. This move underscores the escalating competition between the US and China in the AI sector. The letter aims to solidify alliances and influence the strategic direction of AI development globally. This follows previous efforts by the US to strengthen its position in the AI landscape, emphasizing the geopolitical implications of AI technology. The outcome of this initiative could significantly affect international collaboration in AI research and development, as countries may feel pressured to align with either Washington or Beijing.
