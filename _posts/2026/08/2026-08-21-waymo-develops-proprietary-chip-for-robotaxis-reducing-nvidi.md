Waymo has built its own chip for its robotaxi technology, significantly cutting its reliance on Nvidia. This move marks a strategic shift for Waymo, which previously depended on Nvidia for its chip technology. The development of proprietary hardware could enhance Waymo's ability to optimize performance and reduce costs in its autonomous vehicle operations. This follows a trend in the industry where companies are increasingly investing in custom silicon to gain competitive advantages, as seen in recent investments by Nvidia and others in AI infrastructure. According to [The Decoder](https://the-decoder.com/waymo-builds-its-own-chip-for-its-robotaxis-cutting-its-reliance-on-nvidia/), this development signals a pivotal moment for Waymo as it seeks to strengthen its position in the autonomous vehicle market.
