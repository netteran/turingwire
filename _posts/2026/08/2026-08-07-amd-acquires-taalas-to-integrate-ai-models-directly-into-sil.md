AMD has acquired Taalas, a Canadian startup specializing in the integration of AI models directly into silicon. The acquisition aims to enhance AMD's capabilities in AI hardware, exemplified by a demo chip featuring the Llama 3.1-8B model, which achieves a performance of 16,000 tokens per second per user. This follows AMD's ongoing efforts to innovate in AI hardware, as seen in their recent developments with the MI series accelerators. Notably, Google is reportedly exploring a similar approach for its Gemini AI model, indicating a competitive landscape in AI model integration. This acquisition may enable AMD to offer more efficient AI solutions, directly impacting how developers can leverage hardware for AI applications.
