OpenAI has partnered with CodeAI to launch an initiative aimed at enhancing AI literacy among students. This collaboration focuses on helping students build critical thinking skills regarding AI and developing the ability to use and shape AI responsibly. The initiative is part of a broader effort to prepare the next generation for the challenges and opportunities presented by AI technology. This follows OpenAI's ongoing commitment to education and responsible AI development, as seen in their recent projects and funding efforts. For practitioners, this partnership may influence future educational resources and tools aimed at fostering a more informed user base in AI. More details can be found on the [OpenAI Blog](https://openai.com/index/partnering-with-codeai).
