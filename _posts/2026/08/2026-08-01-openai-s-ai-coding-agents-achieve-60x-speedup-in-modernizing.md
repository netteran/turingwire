OpenAI's AI coding agents can modernize neglected research software with a speedup of 60x. This capability was highlighted in a field report involving academic partners, showcasing how these agents can efficiently update outdated codebases. However, participants in the study noted that while the agents are 'eloquent, convincing, and confidently wrong in ways that are easy to miss', they cannot assess the validity of the underlying science. This limitation suggests that while practitioners can leverage these agents for software modernization, they must still rely on human oversight for scientific accuracy. This follows previous discussions on AI's role in research software, emphasizing the need for careful integration of AI tools in scientific workflows.
