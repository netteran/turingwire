# Hobby Programming Communities Express Hostility Toward LLM Usage

Hobby programming communities, including OSDev, LangDev, TxtDev, EmuDev, RLDev, Demoscene, and code golfers, are increasingly vocal against the use of Language Learning Models (LLMs). These communities, known for their intense gatekeeping and slow progress, argue that relying on LLMs diminishes the value of craftsmanship in programming. An author from the community stated, "Using an LLM to generate the finished piece doesn’t make us craftsmen; it just robs us of the craft." This sentiment reflects a broader concern that expertise in these niche areas offers no protection against being misled by LLM outputs. The backlash against LLMs marks a significant cultural shift within these programming circles, emphasizing a commitment to traditional methods over automated solutions. This follows ongoing discussions in the AI community regarding the implications of LLMs on creativity and skill development, as highlighted in previous coverage on the challenges faced by AI in various domains. For practitioners in these communities, this growing resistance may influence the adoption of LLMs in hobbyist projects and educational contexts.
