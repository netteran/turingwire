OpenAI has launched new education plugins for ChatGPT Work and Codex, targeting K–12 teachers, college educators, and students. These plugins are designed to enhance learning, teaching, research, and building capabilities within educational contexts. The initiative aims to support both primary and secondary education as well as higher education, providing tools that facilitate interactive learning experiences. This follows OpenAI's ongoing commitment to integrating AI into educational frameworks, as seen in previous efforts to modernize research software and tackle complex mathematical problems. The introduction of these plugins could significantly change how educators and students engage with AI tools in their learning environments.
