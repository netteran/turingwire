Meta's new Muse Glimmer model features 30 billion parameters and requires less than 20 GB of memory for its compressed weights. This model is part of a broader strategy by CEO Mark Zuckerberg to reduce restrictions on U.S. labs and enhance competitiveness against firms like OpenAI and Anthropic. An open-weight version of Muse Spark 1.2 is expected to follow soon. This move marks Meta's return to open models, aligning with previous efforts to innovate in AI as seen in their recent developments. The initiative could provide AI engineers with new tools for building applications, especially in environments where memory efficiency is critical.
