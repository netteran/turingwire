Alibaba's Qwen team has released the Qwen 3.8 model, which boasts 27 billion parameters and can process a maximum of 262,000 tokens. This new model outperforms its predecessor, Qwen 3.7 Plus, and is designed for developers focusing on local and agent-based applications. The Qwen 3.8 models are available under the Apache 2.0 license, allowing for broader usage and integration in various projects. This release follows previous developments in the Qwen series, emphasizing Alibaba's commitment to advancing multimodal AI capabilities. The open weights facilitate easier adoption and experimentation for AI practitioners, enhancing the potential for innovative applications in the field.
