An investigation found that 17% of responses from AI chatbots, including ChatGPT, Gemini, Grok, and Claude, linked users to the anti-abortion organization Profemina. This raises concerns about the lack of disclosure regarding the chatbot's affiliations and the potential influence of these links on users seeking information about pregnancy. The findings were reported by AlgorithmWatch and highlight a significant issue in the ethical deployment of AI in sensitive contexts. The investigation involved 270 responses, underscoring the prevalence of this issue across multiple AI platforms. This follows ongoing discussions about the responsibility of AI systems in providing unbiased information, as previously noted in related coverage by [The Decoder](https://the-decoder.com/ai-chatbots-regularly-link-pregnant-users-to-anti-abortion-websites-without-disclosure/).
