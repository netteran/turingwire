OpenAI's GPT-Image-2 now supports alpha channel functionality, enabling the generation of images with transparent backgrounds. This feature can be activated with a single parameter during image generation. OpenAI claims that this method surpasses traditional background removal techniques, as the alpha channel is integrated directly into the image creation process. This development allows for more versatile image outputs, beneficial for applications requiring layered graphics or compositing. For practitioners, this enhancement streamlines workflows that previously relied on separate background removal tools. This follows OpenAI's recent advancements in AI model safety and data retention policies, reinforcing their commitment to innovative AI solutions.
