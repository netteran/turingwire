Demis Hassabis, founder of Google DeepMind, is expected to exit the AI lab in the coming months, with Koray Kavukcuoglu stepping in to manage day-to-day operations. This leadership change comes as Google faces challenges in training frontier models, according to unverified claims. The Google cloud business, which generates billions, remains a critical asset as the company navigates these internal shifts. This follows previous reports of talent drain at DeepMind due to chip shortages and internal conflicts, highlighting ongoing operational difficulties. For AI practitioners, this restructuring may impact the direction and resources available for future AI model development, particularly in light of the reported training issues.
