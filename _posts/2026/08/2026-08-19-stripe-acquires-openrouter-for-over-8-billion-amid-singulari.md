Stripe has acquired OpenRouter for over $8 billion, marking a significant investment in AI capabilities. The acquisition follows Stripe's declaration on January 1 that we are living in the 'beginning of the singularity.' In the first half of the year, Stripe reported a revenue growth of 41%. This acquisition is part of Stripe's strategy to enhance its AI offerings, which could influence how developers integrate AI into payment solutions. The move aligns with current trends in the industry, as noted by claims that 'declaring the singularity is in vogue right now.' This follows Stripe's previous acquisition of OpenRouter, which was covered by [The Decoder](https://the-decoder.com/stripe-declares-were-living-in-the-singularity-and-uses-it-as-a-reason-not-to-ipo/).
