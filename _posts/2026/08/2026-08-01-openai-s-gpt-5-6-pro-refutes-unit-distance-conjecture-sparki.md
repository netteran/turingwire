OpenAI's GPT 5.6 Pro has successfully refuted the Unit Distance Conjecture, marking a significant milestone in AI-assisted mathematics. This model solved two complex problems on its first attempt, showcasing its advanced capabilities. Timothy Gowers, a Fields Medal winner, has expressed concerns about the potential 'destruction of mathematical culture' due to AI's growing role in solving long-standing mathematical problems. This development follows OpenAI's recent advancements in tackling unsolved math problems, reflecting a trend where AI increasingly contributes to significant mathematical breakthroughs. The implications for mathematicians and researchers are profound, as reliance on AI tools could reshape traditional problem-solving approaches.
