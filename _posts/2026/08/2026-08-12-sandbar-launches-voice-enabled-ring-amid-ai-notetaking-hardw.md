Sandbar has launched a voice-enabled ring, joining a growing market for AI notetaking hardware, which has seen significant expansion over the past couple of years. This market includes various device types such as credit-card-sized devices, pendants, pins, and transcribing earbuds. An unnamed source noted that there is a trend towards wearables, particularly rings, as consumers seek to capture fleeting thoughts and ideas. This follows a broader interest in AI-driven hardware solutions, indicating a shift in how users interact with technology for personal note-taking. The introduction of Sandbar's device could influence the design and functionality of future notetaking wearables.
