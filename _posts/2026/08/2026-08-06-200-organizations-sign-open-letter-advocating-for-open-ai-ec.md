In July, NVIDIA signed an open letter advocating for an open AI ecosystem, which has now garnered support from 200 companies and organizations. The letter, titled 'Open Weights and American AI Leadership,' emphasizes that AI leadership should not be defined by a single frontier model but rather by the establishment of an inclusive ecosystem that benefits all sectors. This initiative aligns with NVIDIA's ongoing commitment to fostering collaboration in AI development, as seen in their recent partnerships and investments in U.S. manufacturing and infrastructure. The push for an open ecosystem could influence how AI engineers and researchers approach model development and deployment, promoting interoperability and shared resources. For further details, visit the [NVIDIA Blog](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/open-world-models-physical-ai/).
