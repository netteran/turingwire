OpenAI has announced its new model, Astra, which successfully resolves ten previously unsolved mathematical problems. This development was showcased to policymakers in Washington, highlighting its potential impact on mathematical research and AI applications. Sam Altman, CEO of OpenAI, indicated that the organization has not yet determined whether Astra will be classified as GPT-6 or a variant of GPT-5. The model's capabilities allow multiple agents to tackle complex problems in hours or even days, significantly enhancing problem-solving efficiency. This follows OpenAI's recent advocacy for responsible AI governance amid ongoing discussions about AI development speed and safety, as reported by [The Decoder](https://the-decoder.com/openai-announces-its-next-major-model-astra-by-dropping-ten-previously-unsolved-math-solutions/).
