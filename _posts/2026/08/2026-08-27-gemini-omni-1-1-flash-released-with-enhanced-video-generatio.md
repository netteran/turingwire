On August 27, 2026, Google DeepMind launched Gemini Omni 1.1 Flash, a production-ready update for developers. This model allows for scene extensions in 10-second increments, reaching a total of 40 seconds, and supports high-resolution outputs in 1080p or 4K. Developers can generate 360p previews up to 60% faster than 720p, with the cost of 360p previews being a third of that of standard 720p resolution. Google AI claims that this update provides better control over generative video projects, enabling users to specify start and end frames for smoother transitions and analyze up to 10 seconds of prior context. This release follows the earlier updates in the Gemini series, enhancing the capabilities available on the Google AI Studio and Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform. More details are available on the [Google DeepMind Blog](https://deepmind.google/blog/gemini-omni-1-1-flash-lets-you-build-with-more-control/).
