Demis Hassabis has reportedly stepped back from day-to-day operations at DeepMind for about a year, which coincides with researchers' complaints about limited access to Google’s TPU chips. This situation reflects a broader conflict of interest within Google, which owns the TPU technology that DeepMind relies on. External customers like Anthropic are also affected, as they utilize Google Cloud services that may be impacted by these internal dynamics. The ongoing chip shortages exacerbate these issues, potentially hindering DeepMind's research capabilities. This follows previous coverage of DeepMind's strategic shifts and funding initiatives, highlighting ongoing challenges within the organization. According to [The Decoder](https://the-decoder.com/deepminds-talent-drain-likely-comes-down-to-chip-shortages-a-conflict-of-interest-and-googles-bureaucracy/), these factors contribute to a significant talent drain at DeepMind.
