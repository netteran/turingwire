Sixty percent of senior business and IT leaders, robotics specialists, government, and healthcare officials expect their organizations to operate robot fleets within the next five years. Leaders predict that full-scale robotics deployment could double operational output. Furthermore, 67% believe they will be ready to manage a mixed human-robot workforce by 2030. A significant 74% consider workforce planning and robotics integration important for future operations. This data underscores a growing recognition of robotics' potential, following Intel's previous reports on AI-driven growth and operational enhancements. For practitioners, this indicates a pressing need to develop strategies for integrating robotics into existing workflows.
