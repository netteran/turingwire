OpenAI is funding 14 independent projects that focus on exploring new AI policy ideas to enhance economic opportunity and bolster societal resilience in the Intelligence Age. This initiative aims to address the challenges posed by AI advancements and their societal impacts. The projects are expected to contribute to a more informed policy landscape as AI technologies continue to evolve. This follows OpenAI's recent efforts to engage with AI safety and policy, as seen in their previous dissolution of the Preparedness Team focused on catastrophic AI risks. For practitioners, these funded projects may lead to new frameworks and guidelines that could influence future AI development and deployment strategies. More details are available on the [OpenAI Blog](https://openai.com/index/new-policy-ideas-for-the-intelligence-age).
