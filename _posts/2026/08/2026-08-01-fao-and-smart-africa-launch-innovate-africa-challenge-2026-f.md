The FAO and Smart Africa have launched the Innovate Africa Challenge 2026 to promote AI solutions aimed at enhancing climate-smart agriculture. This initiative is set to mobilize innovative technologies to address agricultural challenges in the Global South. Participants will focus on developing scalable AI applications that can improve food security and sustainability in the region. This follows ongoing efforts by Smart Africa to drive digital transformation across the continent. The challenge is expected to attract startups and innovators looking to leverage AI for agricultural advancements, potentially impacting how agricultural practices are modernized in Africa.
