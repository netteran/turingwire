# Replit Launches Free Mode Powered by GPT-5.6 Luna for Software Creation

Replit has introduced a new Free Mode that allows users to create software without incurring any token costs. This mode is powered by the GPT-5.6 Luna AI model, enabling users to transform ideas into functional software seamlessly. Replit emphasized that this initiative democratizes access to software development tools, making it easier for anyone to participate in coding. This follows Replit's ongoing efforts to enhance accessibility in software creation, aligning with trends in AI-driven development platforms. According to [OpenAI Blog](https://openai.com/index/replit), this move is expected to significantly lower the barrier to entry for aspiring developers and hobbyists alike.
