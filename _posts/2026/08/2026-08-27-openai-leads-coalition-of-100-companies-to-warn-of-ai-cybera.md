OpenAI has rallied over 100 companies, including Microsoft, Google, Anthropic, Deutsche Telekom, and SAP, to sign an open letter warning of imminent AI-powered cyberattacks on critical infrastructure such as hospitals and water treatment plants. The coalition emphasizes the growing sophistication of these potential attacks, which could pose significant risks to public safety and security. This initiative follows previous concerns raised by OpenAI regarding the security implications of rapidly advancing AI technologies, as highlighted in earlier reports. The letter underscores the urgent need for collaboration among tech companies to mitigate these threats, signaling a shift in focus towards cybersecurity in AI development. For practitioners, this coalition may influence future AI governance frameworks and security protocols in product development.
