Google Research has introduced the WikiSkill framework, enabling AI agents to utilize a persistent knowledge base to document both failures and successes. This framework allows AI agents to 'get better over time,' according to Google Research. Larger models benefit more from WikiSkill, while smaller models can match the performance of larger models without it. This development marks a significant step in enhancing the learning capabilities of AI agents, following Google's previous innovations in AI model efficiency and performance. For practitioners, the introduction of WikiSkill may lead to more robust AI systems capable of continuous improvement based on documented experiences.
