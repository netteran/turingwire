## Problem
The paper addresses the gap in reproducibility within hybrid Earth system models that integrate AI techniques. As AI becomes increasingly prevalent in environmental modeling, the complexity introduced can hinder reproducibility, which is essential for validating scientific findings. This work is particularly relevant as it is published as a Perspective in a preprint format, indicating that it has not yet undergone peer review.
