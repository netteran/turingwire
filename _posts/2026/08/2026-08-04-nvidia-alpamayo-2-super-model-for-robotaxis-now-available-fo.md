NVIDIA's Alpamayo 2 Super model for robotaxis and autonomous vehicles is now available for commercial use. This frontier open model addresses complex situations in autonomous driving, emphasizing that handling long-tail events requires more than just object detection and motion prediction. The introduction of Alpamayo 2 Super allows developers to enhance their autonomous vehicle systems with improved capabilities for unpredictable scenarios. This follows NVIDIA's ongoing efforts to advance AI applications in robotics, as highlighted in recent collaborations and product launches. For practitioners, this model expands the tools available for developing robust autonomous vehicle solutions. More details can be found on the [NVIDIA Blog](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/alpamayo-2-super-open-model-now-available/).
