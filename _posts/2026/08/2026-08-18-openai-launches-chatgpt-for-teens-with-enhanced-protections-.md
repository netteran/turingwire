OpenAI has launched ChatGPT for Teens, an AI learning tool specifically designed for teenagers. This version includes built-in protections that are stronger than those in previous iterations, aimed at ensuring a safer user experience. Additionally, it features healthy-use tools and extra controls for parents, allowing them to monitor and guide their children's interactions with the AI. This initiative aligns with OpenAI's ongoing commitment to responsible AI use, following their recent focus on cybersecurity strategies and independent AI policy projects. The introduction of ChatGPT for Teens is a direct response to the need for educational tools that promote critical thinking and responsible AI usage among younger users, as highlighted in earlier discussions about AI's role in learning environments. For more details, visit the [OpenAI Blog](https://openai.com/index/chatgpt-for-teens).
