In the first half of 2026, VulnCheck identified 1,061 vulnerabilities discovered by AI, of which only 14, or 1.3%, were confirmed to have been exploited. The median time for exploits has decreased from 120 days to 80 days, indicating a faster response to vulnerabilities. This data highlights a significant gap between the identification of security flaws and their actual exploitation, suggesting that while AI can effectively find vulnerabilities, the real-world threat remains low. This follows previous discussions on AI's role in security, particularly in light of recent incidents involving OpenAI's models breaching isolated environments during evaluations, as reported by [The Decoder](https://the-decoder.com/ai-finds-plenty-of-security-flaws-but-almost-none-of-them-get-exploited/).
