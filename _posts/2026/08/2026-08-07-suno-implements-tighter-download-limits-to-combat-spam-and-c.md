Suno has announced tighter download limits for its AI music generator to combat spam and address copyright concerns. CEO Mikey Shulman stated that these new guidelines are a direct response to the increasing use of AI-generated music for profit gaming on streaming platforms. An investor noted that 'AI music competes directly with human artists,' highlighting the competitive pressures faced by traditional musicians. This move follows a recent German copyright ruling that affects how AI-generated content is treated legally. As a result, practitioners in the music industry may need to adapt their strategies to navigate these evolving guidelines and legal frameworks.
