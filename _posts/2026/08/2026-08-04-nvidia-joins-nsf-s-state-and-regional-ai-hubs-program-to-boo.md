NVIDIA has joined the U.S. National Science Foundation's (NSF) State and Regional AI Hubs program, which launched today. This initiative aims to expand access to advanced computing, data, software, and expertise necessary for AI-enabled research and education. The program is designed to foster collaboration among academic institutions, industry, and government to enhance AI capabilities across the United States. NVIDIA's involvement is expected to strengthen the infrastructure available for AI research, potentially impacting a wide range of sectors reliant on AI advancements. This follows NVIDIA's recent initiatives in AI applications, including the launch of the Alpamayo 2 Super Model for robotaxis. For more details, visit the [NVIDIA Blog](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/nsf-state-regional-ai-hub-program/).
