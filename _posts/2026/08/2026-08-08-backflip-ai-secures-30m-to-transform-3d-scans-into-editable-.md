Backflip AI has raised $30 million to enhance its AI model that converts 3D scans into editable CAD models, significantly reducing the time required for this process from hours to minutes. Founded three years ago, Backflip AI aims to address the fact that, as CEO Greg Mark states, most factories have digital models for less than one percent of their parts. The new funding will support integration with Autodesk Fusion, a widely used software tool in the CAD space, enabling broader access to digital modeling capabilities. This development follows a trend in the industry towards improving digital asset availability and efficiency in manufacturing processes. For practitioners, this means enhanced capabilities in creating and editing CAD models from 3D scans, potentially transforming workflows in design and production.
