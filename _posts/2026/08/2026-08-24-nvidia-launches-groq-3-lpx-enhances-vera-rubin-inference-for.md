NVIDIA has announced the full production of Groq 3 LPX, enhancing the Vera Rubin NVL72 AI inference system. This development aims to improve fast token generation for agentic systems, crucial for advanced AI applications. NVIDIA claims that the future of AI inference will not rely on a single breakthrough chip, network, or system. This follows NVIDIA's recent focus on AI factories as critical infrastructure for the AI era, highlighting a strategic shift in their approach to AI development. Practitioners can expect improved performance in AI inference tasks with the integration of Groq 3 LPX into their systems, as detailed in the [NVIDIA Blog](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/vera-rubin-lpx-spectrum-x-nvlink-fusion/).
