Apple's revamped Siri AI is now built on Google's Gemini model, but it is currently unavailable in the EU. Early testers have praised Siri's ability to handle multi-step requests and utilize screen context effectively. However, they also reported issues such as hallucinations and gaps in personal context. This follows Apple's ongoing efforts to enhance Siri's capabilities, which have been delayed for years. The reliance on Google's Gemini marks a significant shift in Apple's approach to AI integration, particularly in light of its previous infrastructure strategies. For developers, the absence of Siri in the EU may limit their ability to leverage this new functionality in that market.
