Google's AI search has ceased providing emergency advice when users report being alone with individuals from specific nationalities, including African, Indian, or Pakistani. Previously, the AI would prompt users with a message stating, "Please get to a safe place or call emergency services." This change raises concerns about the AI's handling of sensitive situations and its potential biases. The decision may affect users seeking urgent help, as the AI's guidance is now less comprehensive. This follows ongoing scrutiny of AI systems and their responses to emergency contexts, highlighting the need for careful consideration of how nationalities are factored into AI decision-making.
