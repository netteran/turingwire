In 2023, IBM announced its collaboration with Confluent to integrate time series models into Confluent's real-time data streaming platform. This partnership aims to address the needs of the 80% of organizations that have adopted real-time data for decision-making since 2021. IBM claims that organizations utilizing real-time data have reported a 3x increase in operational efficiency and that 50% have seen improved customer experiences. As IBM states, "Real-time data is essential for modern businesses." This collaboration follows IBM's previous efforts to scale AI solutions, as seen in their strategic partnership with Google Cloud earlier in June 2026. The integration of these models is expected to enhance the capabilities of businesses relying on real-time analytics, enabling them to make more informed decisions rapidly.
