AlgorithmWatch analyzed 4,480 election-related search queries on Google, revealing that Google's election AI Overviews are opaque, rely on few sources, and sometimes take sides. This analysis was conducted under the EU's Digital Services Act, highlighting concerns about the transparency and reliability of information provided by Google's algorithms. The findings suggest that practitioners relying on Google's AI for election-related content may encounter biased or incomplete information, impacting the quality of insights derived from these tools. This follows previous scrutiny of Google's AI models, including the recent introduction of WikiSkill for AI agents to enhance learning from mistakes, indicating ongoing challenges in AI transparency.
