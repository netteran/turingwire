Recent research indicates that engaging with the Google Gemini chatbot for approximately seven minutes can significantly diminish conspiracy beliefs regarding contemporary crises. This finding suggests that conversational AI may be more effective than static information sources, such as fact sheets, in addressing misinformation. The study highlights the potential of AI-driven interactions to influence belief systems positively, even in scenarios where verified information is scarce.

The experiments conducted demonstrated that the reduction in conspiracy beliefs was not only immediate but also persisted over time. Follow-up surveys conducted weeks later revealed that participants maintained lower levels of conspiracy beliefs, extending even to unrelated events. This suggests that the impact of the chatbot interaction may have broader implications for belief modification beyond the initial context.

These findings underscore the efficacy of conversational agents in combating misinformation and suggest a promising avenue for future research in the field of AI and public discourse. The implications of this study could inform strategies for using AI in educational and informational contexts to mitigate the spread of conspiracy theories.
