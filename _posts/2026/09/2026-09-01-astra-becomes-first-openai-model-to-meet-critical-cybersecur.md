Astra is the first OpenAI model to meet the Critical cybersecurity capability threshold under the Preparedness Framework. This release signifies a notable advancement in AI safety, as Astra incorporates stronger safeguards compared to previous models. The Preparedness Framework aims to ensure that AI systems are robust against cybersecurity threats, which is increasingly vital as AI applications expand across various sectors. This development follows OpenAI's ongoing commitment to enhancing security measures in AI, as seen in their recent warnings regarding AI model vulnerabilities. Practitioners can now leverage Astra for applications requiring heightened cybersecurity assurance. For further details, visit the [OpenAI Blog](https://openai.com/index/path-to-astra).
