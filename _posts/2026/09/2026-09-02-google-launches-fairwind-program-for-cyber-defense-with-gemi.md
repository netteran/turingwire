Google has launched the **Fairwind Program**, a limited access initiative aimed at enhancing cybersecurity for governments and trusted partners, utilizing the **Gemini 3.8 Flash Cyber** model. This program is part of a broader commitment, with **$100 million** allocated globally for cybersecurity efforts, including **$36 million** specifically for **35 cyber clinics**. So far, **1,250** entities, including hospitals, public school districts, and municipal utilities, have benefited from this funding. The program aims to evolve alongside user needs, with Google stating, "The Fairwind Program will evolve alongside our partners and users' needs." Additionally, the **CodeMender** tool will be employed to identify and rectify vulnerabilities, emphasizing the importance of reducing the time between flaw detection and patching. This follows Google's recent release of **Gemini 3.8 Flash** on **September 2, 2026**, which is part of their ongoing efforts to bolster cybersecurity capabilities. For more details, refer to the [Google DeepMind Blog](https://deepmind.google/blog/proactive-cyber-defense-for-governments-and-enterprises/).
