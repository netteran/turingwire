A recent survey by ACM involving 763 computer science educators from 49 countries found that 68% have changed their exams due to the influence of AI. Nearly half of the respondents reported lacking proven examples for effectively integrating AI into their curricula. This shift indicates a transition in teaching focus, as one claim suggests, "Teaching is moving from writing code to understanding it." This follows ongoing discussions in the industry about AI's role in education and assessment methods, highlighting the need for educators to adapt their evaluation strategies in light of emerging technologies. For practitioners, this underscores a critical need to develop new assessment frameworks that accurately reflect students' understanding in an AI-augmented learning environment.
