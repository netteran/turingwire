Dario Amodei, CEO of Anthropic, warned that open AI models pose risks of misuse for biological or cyberattacks. He expressed concerns that authoritarian states, particularly China, could surpass the U.S. in AI capabilities if these models remain unregulated. Amodei clarified, "I have never called for a ban," emphasizing the need for responsible management rather than outright prohibition. This statement follows Anthropic's recent focus on balancing innovation with safety in AI development, as highlighted in previous coverage of their revenue growth and product performance. Practitioners should note the increasing emphasis on risk management in AI model deployment, as articulated by industry leaders like Amodei.
