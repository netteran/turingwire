Claude Opus 5 has achieved a score of 61 points in the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index, making it the top performer in this benchmarking index, as reported by [The Decoder](https://the-decoder.com/anthropics-claude-opus-5-costs-well-below-fable-5-while-matching-or-beating-it-across-most-benchmarks/). The model excels in analytical quality and coding capabilities, outperforming Claude Fable 5 while costing up to half as much at lower reasoning tiers. This development indicates a competitive pricing strategy from Anthropic, positioning Claude Opus 5 as a viable alternative for organizations looking to leverage advanced AI capabilities without incurring high costs. The race at the top remains close, particularly with competitors like GPT-5.6 Sol also vying for market share.
