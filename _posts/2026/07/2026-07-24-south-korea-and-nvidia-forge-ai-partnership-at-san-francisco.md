# South Korea and NVIDIA Forge AI Partnership at San Francisco Summit

South Korean President Jae Myung Lee announced a strategic partnership with NVIDIA during the AI Summit in San Francisco last month. This collaboration aims to enhance South Korea's AI capabilities, leveraging NVIDIA's technology to drive innovation across various sectors. Jensen Huang, NVIDIA's Founder and CEO, emphasized the importance of this partnership in advancing AI research and development in the region. 

This initiative follows Huang's visit to Korea last month, where discussions focused on integrating NVIDIA's AI solutions into South Korea's technological landscape. The partnership is expected to impact multiple industries, including healthcare and manufacturing, by providing access to advanced AI tools and infrastructure. 

Practitioners in South Korea can anticipate new opportunities for deploying NVIDIA's AI technologies, which may accelerate the development of AI-driven applications. For more details, see the full report on [NVIDIA Blog](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/ai-summit-korea-partners-and-nvidia/).
