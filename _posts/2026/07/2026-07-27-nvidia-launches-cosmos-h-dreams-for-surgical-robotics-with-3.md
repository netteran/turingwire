NVIDIA has launched Cosmos-H-Dreams, a software tool designed for surgical robotics, after five years of development. This initiative is part of a $1 billion investment in the surgical robotics sector. The new tool is expected to increase the efficiency of surgical procedures by 30%. Widespread adoption of surgical robotics is anticipated by 2025. An NVIDIA executive stated that "Cosmos-H-Dreams will revolutionize surgical procedures." This follows NVIDIA's ongoing efforts in AI and robotics, including their recent partnership with South Korea to advance AI technologies. For practitioners, the introduction of Cosmos-H-Dreams could significantly enhance the capabilities and efficiency of surgical robotics systems, enabling more precise and effective surgical interventions.
