The Delhi High Court ruled that OpenAI's AI training is classified as private use, granting a significant win in its copyright dispute against the Indian news agency ANI. This ruling comes as part of a larger legal battle, with the main trial still pending. The court's decision specifically pertains to articles published after the models were trained, indicating a nuanced interpretation of copyright in the context of AI. This follows previous coverage of OpenAI's legal challenges and highlights ongoing tensions between AI developers and content creators. The ruling could influence how AI companies approach training data and copyright compliance in India.
