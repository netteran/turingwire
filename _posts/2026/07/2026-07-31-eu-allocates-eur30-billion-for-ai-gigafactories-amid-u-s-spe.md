The European Commission has announced a funding initiative of up to €30 billion for AI gigafactories. In stark contrast, major U.S. tech companies are projected to spend $600 billion on computing infrastructure in the current year, which is 20 times more than the EU's investment. This disparity highlights the differing approaches to AI infrastructure development between the two regions. Practitioners in the AI sector may need to consider the implications of this funding gap on the availability and scalability of AI resources in Europe versus the U.S. This follows previous discussions on the need for global coordination in AI research advancements, as reported by [The Decoder](https://the-decoder.com/eu-pools-up-to-e30-billion-for-ai-gigafactories-while-us-tech-giants-casually-spend-20-times-more/).
