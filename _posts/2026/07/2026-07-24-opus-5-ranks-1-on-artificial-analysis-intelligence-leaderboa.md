Opus 5 is currently ranked #1 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Leaderboard, evaluated among 586 models. It surpasses Claude Fable 5, which holds the second position, and GPT-5.6 Sol, in third. The leaderboard utilizes the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index v4.1, which includes nine evaluation metrics such as GDPval-AA v2 and AA-Omniscience. Notably, Mercury 2 is recognized as the fastest model, processing at 821 tokens per second, while HyperNova 60B follows at 429 tokens per second. For latency, Gemini 2.5 Flash-Lite leads with 0.34 seconds, and Command A+ has a latency of 0.42 seconds. The Llama 4 Scout model boasts the largest context window at 10 million tokens. This ranking provides a clear benchmark for AI developers seeking to optimize model selection based on performance metrics.
