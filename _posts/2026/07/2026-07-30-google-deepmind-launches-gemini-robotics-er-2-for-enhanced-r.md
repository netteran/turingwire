Google DeepMind has introduced Gemini Robotics ER 2, a robotics software tool designed to enhance video understanding, task orchestration, and multi-robot collaboration. This tool enables robots to reason, collaborate, and solve real-world tasks more effectively. The advancements in Gemini Robotics ER 2 represent a significant step change in how robots can work together in complex environments. This follows DeepMind's ongoing efforts to integrate AI capabilities in robotics, as seen in their previous initiatives like the ATL Saathi Program aimed at empowering innovators in India. Practitioners can leverage this new tool to improve the efficiency and effectiveness of robotic systems in various applications, according to [Google DeepMind Blog](https://deepmind.google/blog/gemini-robotics-er-2-powering-robotics-with-video-understanding-task-orchestration-and-multi-robot-collaboration/).
