The Open Secure AI Alliance has been established to focus on enhancing AI safety and security through open source software. This initiative highlights that cybersecurity is among the top three beneficiaries of open source technology. The alliance emphasizes the importance of open source software as a critical pillar of the global economy, according to claims made by the organization. This development aligns with previous discussions on AI safety and security, particularly in the context of partnerships like the one between South Korea and NVIDIA reported earlier this year. Practitioners can expect increased collaboration and resources aimed at improving the safety and security of AI systems. For more details, visit the [NVIDIA Blog](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/open-secure-ai-alliance/).
